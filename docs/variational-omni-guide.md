# Variational (Omni) — Early Project Breakdown

**Category:** Perpetual DEX · **Chain:** Arbitrum · **Stage:** Mainnet (private beta), pre-token
**Official site:** https://variational.io · **App:** https://omni.variational.io · **X:** @variational_io

## Why it stands out

- **Airdrop confirmed** in the published $VAR tokenomics (24 Sep 2026): **32% of the total $VAR supply goes to points holders**
- **100% unlocked at TGE** — no vesting cliff on the airdrop allocation
- TGE expected **Q4 2026**
- Backers include Bain Capital Crypto, Peak XV, Coinbase Ventures, Dragonfly and Hack VC. Reported funding totals differ by source ($11.8M vs $61.8M cumulative), so treat the exact raise as unconfirmed — the investor list is consistent across sources.

## How the points program works

- **150,000 points distributed every week** until TGE
- Earn points by depositing USDC, trading, and holding open interest (OI) on Omni
- Eligibility floor: earn at least **1 point** and sign the $VAR terms when prompted

## Steps to start

1. Open the official app: https://omni.variational.io (private beta — access may be limited)
2. Connect a wallet on Arbitrum and deposit USDC
3. Trade perps and hold sensible open interest — points accrue weekly
4. Keep at least 1 point and complete the $VAR terms signature before TGE

## Risks — read before farming

- This needs real capital: perpetual trading carries liquidation risk. Never farm with money you cannot afford to lose.
- Private beta means access and rules can change without notice.
- The value of points depends on the $VAR price at TGE, which is unknown.
