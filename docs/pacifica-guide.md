# Pacifica (Solana Perp DEX) — Points & Airdrop Watch Guide

**Project:** Pacifica — perpetuals DEX on Solana
**Site:** https://pacifica.fi
**X:** @pacifica_fi
**Chain:** Solana
**Token status:** No token announced (as of the 2 Nov 2026 research check)

## Why it is on the watchlist

- One of the largest perp DEXs on Solana — around $1B in daily volume and roughly $300B cumulative volume by 19 Oct 2026
- Self-funded, no VC money — unusual for a project of this size
- Co-founder Constance Wang, former FTX COO

## Points program

- 10M points distributed every week
- Snapshots every Thursday 00:00 UTC
- Points are earned from trading activity on the platform

## Airdrop status — SPECULATIVE, read this carefully

- Pacifica has NOT announced a token, a TGE date, or a points-to-token conversion in its official documentation (checked 11 Oct – 2 Nov 2026)
- A ~30% community allocation figure circulates via aggregators as a team statement — it is NOT confirmed in primary docs
- Treat points as a possible future reward, never a promised payout

## How to participate

1. Go to https://pacifica.fi and connect a Solana wallet
2. Deposit collateral and trade perps to earn weekly points
3. Be consistent — points are snapshotted every Thursday 00:00 UTC

## Risks (the honest section)

- Perpetuals can liquidate you — real capital is at risk
- Points may never convert to a token; time and fees spent are gone either way
- Start small, and never farm with money you cannot afford to lose
