# Security Alerts — Clinton | Crypto Professor

Verified crypto security alerts and major follow-ups. Facts are taken from official sources or two independent reputable sources; figures are as the sources state them.

## 2026-11-15 — Coinone customer data leak via support provider MetaM

- Coinone confirmed customer personal data leaked after a hack of MetaM, its outsourced customer-support provider.
- Exposed (per customer support chats): names, phone numbers, emails, dates of birth, bank accounts, passwords, resident registration numbers, IDs, home addresses, wallet addresses and transaction IDs.
- Coinone's own account, trading and wallet systems show no direct breach.
- Safety: if you are a Coinone user, change your password and watch for targeted phishing.
- Sources: The Herald Business, Financial News, News1; Yonhap/DailySecu.

## 2026-11-15 — Kava Lend exploit: confirmed figures, $612K blocked

- The Block and Bitquery now agree: ~55.2M KAVA (~$3.76M at the pre-attack price) was drained from Kava Lend via a dormant BUSD market whose interest index was inflated 243,000x in 3 minutes, borrowing in 127 loans.
- ~49.2M KAVA was bridged to BNB Chain and sold for ~$2.1M; the Relay bridge blocked 612,416 USDT (29% of the proceeds).
- Kava has still not published an official loss figure or post-mortem; the Upbit, Bithumb and Coinone deposit/withdrawal suspensions and the Axelar bridge pause stand.
- Sources: The Block, Bitquery.

## 2026-11-14 — Kava Lend security incident confirmed by Kava

- Kava confirmed abnormal activity in Kava Lend: KAVA was bridged to BSC and sold. Kava said there was no infinite minting and the impact was contained in Kava Lend.
- Upbit put KAVA under a trading warning and suspended deposits/withdrawals; Bithumb also suspended them. KAVA fell ~46% in 24 hours.
- Safety: do not interact with suspicious Kava links; follow only official Kava channels.
- Sources: Kava official X via KuCoin/ChainCatcher, PANews, CryptoNews, TokenPost.

## 2026-11-14 — Unocoin confirms ~$200K loss, backup keys compromised

- Unocoin's official update estimates the loss from its Nov 6 incident at ~USD 200,000: 3 of its 20+ hot wallets were compromised via backup keys held in its own custody; BitGo's infrastructure was not compromised.
- Unocoin will cover the loss in full from its own resources; no customer balances were affected. A portion of the affected funds has been frozen.
- Customer data may be exposed (emails, residential addresses, encrypted passwords, KYC-related information, balances) — watch for targeted phishing. Unocoin never asks for passwords, OTPs or recovery phrases.
- Source: Unocoin official blog.

## 2026-11-14 — Binance recovery update: withdrawals reopened on 87 networks

- After the $6.3B Oct 29 hack, Binance reopened withdrawals on 87 networks this week (including APT, HYPE, ETHW, LTC, NEAR, LINEA, MEGAETH, ADA, STARKNET, ICP, AVAX, FIL), with full restoration targeted before next weekend.
- User assets were already fully covered from company funds.
- Safety: check withdrawal status only on Binance's official pages.
- Sources: Binance announcement via TokenPost, Lookonchain.

## 2026-11-14 — Web3Auth update: logins resumed, Torus Wallet also affected

- After the Nov 6 Keplr/Web3Auth private-key breach (at least $1.82M stolen), Web3Auth has resumed logins and wallet access for existing users (Plug & Play and Single Factor Auth SDKs); Torus Wallet was also affected.
- Rescued funds are held securely but not yet returned; return-process details are coming. SDK v9 and older are deprecated — upgrade to v10/v11.
- Safety: follow only official Web3Auth/Keplr channels; ignore unsolicited "recovery" offers.
- Source: Web3Auth official incident update page.

## 2026-11-14 — ScreenConnect fake-session campaign (Huntress)

- Huntress reported attackers planted 2,408 unauthorized guest sessions on at least 118 self-hosted ScreenConnect servers since Nov 3, disguised with crypto-exchange-themed machine names (e.g. KUCOIN-SRV). No exchange was breached and no crypto funds loss has been detected.
- Safety: update ScreenConnect to 26.6.5 or later and revoke unknown sessions.
- Sources: Huntress report via The Hacker News, MSSP Alert; ConnectWise Trust Center.

## 2026-11-13 — MANTRA freezes new unbonding; Upbit & Bithumb to delist OM

- After hackers hit Keplr/Web3Auth wallets and unstaked victims' staked OM without consent, MANTRA suspended new unbonding for 7 days; several hacker wallets were frozen via Governance Proposal 41. Unbondings already in progress are not paused — affected stakers must redelegate before the 8-day window ends.
- Upbit and Bithumb announced they will delist MANTRA (OM) on 14 Dec 2026; withdrawals remain open until mid-January 2027.
- Sources: MANTRA official X post; CoinEdition, TokenPost.
