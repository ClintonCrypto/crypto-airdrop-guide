# Wallet Safety for Airdrop Hunters

Airdrop hunting means connecting wallets to new apps, testnets and claim pages every week. Most losses don't come from a bad project — they come from a bad wallet setup. This checklist keeps your funds separate from your farming.

## 1. Use three wallets, not one

- **Vault wallet** — holds funds you never want to lose. Never connects to airdrop sites, testnets or claim pages. Hardware wallet if possible.
- **Farming wallet** — your daily airdrop wallet. Holds only what you need for gas and tasks.
- **Burner wallet** — for brand-new, unverified sites. Fund it with the minimum, drain it after.

Never keep your main savings in the wallet you connect to new projects.

## 2. Seed phrase rules

- Write it on paper, store it offline, in two separate safe places.
- Never type it into a website, form, DM or "support" chat. No real project ever asks for it.
- Never screenshot it or store it in cloud notes, email or chat apps.
- If a seed phrase has ever touched the internet, move the funds to a fresh wallet.

## 3. Social-login wallets need extra care

- Wallets created with Google/Apple login depend on that login provider's security, not just yours. In Nov 2026, Keplr confirmed unauthorized transactions on accounts created via Google/Apple login after a Web3Auth incident — seed-phrase and hardware wallets were not affected.
- Prefer seed-phrase or hardware wallets for anything holding real value.
- If you used social login, don't send new funds to that account until the provider confirms the issue is resolved, and don't re-import it elsewhere on advice from a DM.

## 4. Before you connect to any site

- Check the URL yourself — bookmark official sites, never click links from DMs or search ads.
- New project? Check its official X account, docs and backers first.
- Revoke old approvals regularly (revoke.cash or your wallet's approval manager).
- One approval at a time — never approve "unlimited" token spend unless you understand why.

## 5. If something goes wrong

- Move remaining funds to a fresh wallet immediately — don't wait to understand the attack.
- Revoke the suspicious approval.
- Report it through the project's official channels only.
- Never trust DMs offering to "help recover" your funds — that's the second scam.

## Quick checklist before every session

- [ ] I'm using my farming or burner wallet, not my vault wallet
- [ ] The site URL matches the official one I bookmarked
- [ ] I know exactly what I'm approving
- [ ] My seed phrase stays offline
